SpiceGarden demo guide

An AI phone agent that takes food orders and table bookings in Indian languages. Everything shows up on the live dashboard.

The 5-minute story

  1. Sign in and open Orders, Tables and Calls side by side (phone or second screen).
  2. Place an order with the voice agent: ask for 2 dishes, give a name, confirm the number.
  3. Watch the new order appear on Orders within a few seconds. Click Start cooking, then Mark completed.
  4. Book a table: 4 guests tomorrow at 8 PM. Open Tables to see the floor plan colour the table.
  5. The caller's phone gets a WhatsApp confirmation with the Order or Booking ID.
  6. Open Calls to see the call summary linked to the order or booking it produced.

What to say to the agent

Order: “I'd like two Paneer Butter Masala, four Butter Naan and one Mango Lassi. Pickup. My name is Ravi.”

Booking: “Table for four tomorrow at 8 PM, name Ravi.”

Hindi / Telugu: the agent answers in the caller's language. Try the same order in Hindi.

  • Use your own mobile number as the caller number so the WhatsApp message reaches you.
  • Bookings are for 12:00–2:00 PM and 7:00–9:30 PM (90-minute slots). The largest table seats 8.
